Location

The seed bank and the in vitro collection are located at two sites in Madrid’s Ciudad Universitaria.

Main site — seed bank

The main site of the UPM Plant Germplasm Bank (seed bank and office) is located at the ETS of Agronomic, Food and Biosystems Engineering, of the Universidad Politécnica de Madrid (UPM), in Ciudad Universitaria.

In vitro collection

The in vitro collection is located in the Department of Plant Biology, within the Biotechnology and Plant Biology Building of the Universidad Complutense de Madrid (UCM), Calle José Antonio Novais, 12, Ciudad Universitaria, 28040 Madrid.

The Germplasm Bank comprises a seed bank, an in vitro conservation unit and a field collections unit. It currently conserves close to 10,000 seed accessions from 3,500 different species.
